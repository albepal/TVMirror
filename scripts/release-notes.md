AirPlay-style screen mirroring from a Mac or Windows PC to a Google TV / Android TV or Fire TV (Fire OS 7+).

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `TVMirror-mac.dmg` | Mac (macOS 14+, Apple Silicon and Intel). Open it and drag TVMirror to Applications. |
| `TVMirror-windows.exe` | Windows 10/11 (64-bit). Portable: just run it; it appears next to the clock. |
| `TVMirror-tv.apk` | The TV app (Google TV / Android TV 8+, Fire TV with Fire OS 7+). Install it once on the TV. |

## Setup

1. **TV:** install the free app **Downloader** from Google Play, open it, enter
   `github.com/albepal/TVMirror/releases/latest/download/TVMirror-tv.apk` and install. Open TVMirror on the TV.
   (Optional: press **Allow it to start from anywhere** on its home screen so mirroring can start from any app.)
2. **Computer:** open TVMirror. A setup guide walks you through the rest and finds your TV.

**First launch:** these builds aren't signed by Apple/Microsoft.
- Mac: if macOS says it can't check the app, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**; then allow *Screen & System Audio Recording* when asked.
- Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Mirror or extend
- **Mirror Screen** – the TV shows your screen.
- **Extend Screen** (Mac) – the TV becomes a second display you can drag windows onto.

## Modes
- **Responsive** – lowest delay, for mouse and typing.
- **Smooth** – holds the picture ~150 ms to ride out Wi-Fi pauses; best for videos, and keeps sound in sync.
